# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people. Assume good intent while naming concrete impact. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may limit participation when needed to protect the project. Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel once one is published in `SECURITY.md`; do not post sensitive personal details publicly.

This project welcomes people of every background and experience level. Technical explanations should help others participate rather than gatekeep them.
