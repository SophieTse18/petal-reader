    How to use Petal Reader

    Reading

    • Paste or type text (formatting, colours and code highlighting are kept), or Open file / drag in a .md, .txt or .html file.
    • Press Play. The spoken word glows yellow and the current sentence is softly tinted.
    • Double-click any word to read from exactly there. Single clicks never move your place, so you can select and edit freely.
    • Drag the time bar, scroll your mouse wheel over it, or use the 30-second buttons. Marks on the bar are your headings. Hover a mark to see its name and time.

    Keyboard

    Ctrl + EnterPlay / pause (works while typing) SpacePlay / pause (when not typing) Ctrl + [   Ctrl + ]Back / forward 30 seconds ← →On the time bar: back / forward 5 seconds EscPause

    Getting the most human voice

    • Microsoft Edge gives free “Natural” voices (Natasha and William for Australian English, Ava, Andrew, Emma and Brian for US English, and more). They sound the most human but need internet.
    • Offline, the browser uses the voices installed on your computer. If the internet drops mid-read, Petal Reader switches to an offline voice automatically.
    • Times are estimates. They get more accurate as you listen, because Petal Reader learns how fast each voice really speaks.

    Privacy

    • Your text stays in this browser and is saved automatically on this computer. Online voices send the text being read to Microsoft or Google to be spoken.
